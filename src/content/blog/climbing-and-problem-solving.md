When you're stuck on a climbing problem, the worst thing you can do is keep throwing yourself at the same sequence hoping it'll stick. You have to step back, watch someone else, or just rest and come back with fresh eyes.

Debugging is the same. The moment I step away from the terminal and explain the problem out loud — even to nobody — something usually clicks.

Both require you to trust the process over the outcome. You don't force a crux move, and you don't force a solution.
